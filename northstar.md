City Social is like the best parts of Craigslist with the feature richness and interaction richness and visual richness of a modern social networking site like Facebook or Instagram.
The best parts of Craigslist are
- You can connect with strangers in your city.
- It mostly serves a utilitarian purpose, like you're trying to get some utility out of the interaction.
- You make real connections with people.
- It's not an attention hog. It's really trying to help you accomplish what you want quickly.

So city social is like has all those goals but with the technology advancements and usability of a Facebook or Instagram.

You can also think of City Social as kind of like a modern-day newspaper designed for a specific city obviously but it's a newspaper in that it covers everything and helps you do anything in the city that might be worthwhile. It's like Google News, dedicated to one city, with social networking features and a social network graph.
